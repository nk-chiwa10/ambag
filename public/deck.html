The problem

A group buy, twenty people, and nobody can prove who paid.

  • Payments arrive as screenshots in a group chat. The ledger is the chat.
  • The treasurer rebuilds it in a spreadsheet. Names, nicknames, partial payments.
  • If the vendor needs the full amount or the order dies, someone fronts the money.
  • On the deadline, the question is simple: is the target actually met?
AmbAG2 / 6
What it does

Money on-chain. Names off-chain. A human signs.

The contract
Target · deadline · fixed vendor

Holds tBNB. Releases to the vendor only at target. After a missed deadline, each member claims a refund.

The AI
Transcribes, never decides

Reads the messy list, app matches refs and amounts against the chain, flags duplicates and short payments. The treasurer confirms.

Public money  The chain stores a 6-character ref, never a name.

Why a chain

The rule has to hold when the treasurer is asleep.

  • Custody — funds sit in the contract, not in a personal account.
  • Permissionless release — anyone can trigger the payout once the target is met.
  • Pull refunds — deadline missed, each member claims their own money.
  • Proof — verified contract, public ledger, private roster.

The escrow is the commodity. The reconciliation is the product.
